# What-if readiness — optional historical sensitivity

Six independent disconnected parameters are fully prepared: purchase materials, inbound freight, outbound freight, labor, energy and net selling prices. Each GENERATESERIES table spans −50% to +100% by one percentage point, default 0%; this is a UI exploration range, not an economic forecast or changed assumption. Base data and measures never reference scenario parameters. No scenario or Power BI model has been executed in Phase 9A.

Scenario COGS scales each recognized historical component independently; overhead stays at baseline. Outbound scales only shipment distribution expense. Selling-price changes scale net revenue at unchanged quantity/discount behavior. Fixed selling and administration remain unchanged; variable selling scales with revised revenue. Idle and fully defective production expense remain at baseline. The resulting scenario OP is company-only. All cost changes are applied once, and the six zero settings must reproduce base results.

These formulas are static cost/revenue exposure tests. They do not rebuild purchases, monthly weighted averages, inventory timing, price elasticity, capacity, labor hours or production volumes. A labor-rate change affects the historical blended labor COGS component (including its support-cost share); it is not a flexible-budget staffing model. Energy acts on the blended recognized energy component, not a newly recalculated activity/standby ledger. Freight is recognized on historical COGS or shipment expense according to component ownership. No supplier/material-specific finished-goods lineage exists, so a DimMaterial filter cannot implement a material-specific sales COGS forecast.

For zero-parameter validation run validation/06_optional_zero_scenario.dax. Then test one parameter at a time, holding all others at zero: material/inbound/labor/energy increases lower GP/OP by their selected historical component times the change; outbound changes lower OP but leave GP unchanged; price changes raise revenue and variable selling at fixed volume. Check combined scenarios equal the sum of their linear isolated effects within final display rounding. Scenario monetary results are sensitivities, not regenerated cent-reconciled accounting records.

A full prospective forecast would need inventory/purchasing timing, material-level lineage, volume/capacity drivers and demand-response assumptions approved separately. It cannot be claimed ready from these static measures. No additional development is needed for the supplied six-parameter static sensitivity dashboard; the prospective forecast is a different scope.
